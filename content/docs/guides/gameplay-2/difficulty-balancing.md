## 1. What is Difficulty and Balancing?

Difficulty and Balancing are two different things, however they tie in to eachother very well. Difficulty is simply how hard or easy you want your level to be, and balancing is avoiding Broken Gameplay, or in other words, making parts of your level too hard or too easy compared to the intended difficulty. For Platformer Mode, "Broken Gameplay" could mean broken mechanics, overpowered items, and much more. It is important to remember that gameplay should always be balanced, no matter what. If something feels too overpowered, too weak, or too easy/difficult for your level, you will want to fix that.

## 2. Factors For Difficulty


Difficulty isn't just one thing on its own. Instead, it is comprised of many factors that each contribute to the overall difficulty of the level. In this section, we will go over the 4 main ones that will contribute most to the difficulty of your level.

#### Mechanical Difficulty
Mechanical Difficulty is the difficulty of performing mechanics in your level. This can be as simple as an orb chain or a fast gamemode switch, or as complicated as a full minigame in your level or 3D movement. Anything that players interact with in your level is a mechanic, and Mechanical Difficulty is the difficulty of using them.

#### Timing
Timing is the amount of time you have to preform an input. Usually, the shorter time you have, the harder the input is. You've probably heard of the term "frame perfect" before. This is a term used to describe a timing that you have a very short amount of time to perform, thus making the timing very hard. Timings can be part of Mechanical Difficulty because in order to use a mechanic you must perform a timing.

#### Length
Length is the amount of time the player has to be continuously performing inputs. For example, a 10 *second* long level with incredibly hard timings would be **easier** than a 10 *minute* long level also with super hard timings simply because the player might be more tired when performing inputs in a 10 minute long level.
